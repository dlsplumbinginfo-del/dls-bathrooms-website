Remote bathroom estimate

No time for a home visit? Start with a clear video.

If you are busy, planning ahead or simply want a realistic figure before committing, send DLS a slow walk-through video, clear photographs and rough room dimensions.

We can review the existing room, proposed layout and finish level, then prepare a useful provisional estimate for either labour-only or supply-and-fit. Supply-and-fit projects may need a few extra details about products, tiles and finishes.

Request a Video EstimateNo initial home appointment needed

What to send

  1. A slow room walk-throughShow the floor, ceiling, windows, doors and every wall.
  2. Clear photographsInclude the current bath, shower, toilet, basin and visible pipework.
  3. Rough dimensionsRoom length, width, ceiling height and the main fixture sizes.
  4. Your preferred scopeTell us what is moving and choose labour-only or supply-and-fit.
What you receive

A realistic planning figure with the assumptions, likely options and next steps made clear. Where the information is complete, DLS may be able to prepare a written quotation remotely.

Remote estimates are based on the information supplied. The final written quotation may change if the scope, product choices, measurements or site conditions differ, and a visit may still be needed before work begins.

Common questions

Useful answers before you enquire.

Do you cover Stockport and Manchester?

Yes. DLS Bathrooms Ltd provides complete bathroom renovations across Stockport, Manchester and surrounding areas. Send your postcode and we will confirm availability.

Which bathroom trades does DLS manage?

DLS coordinates the complete installation, including preparation, tiling, plumbing, bathroom electrics, joinery and final fitting.

Can I get an initial estimate using photographs and video?

Yes. Send a slow room walk-through, clear photographs, rough dimensions and your preferred finish for a provisional remote estimate.

Can I see my chosen products in the room before work starts?

Yes. DLS can create a product-matched bathroom visualisation using photographs and measurements of your room together with links or product codes for your chosen tiles, furniture, brassware, lighting and other finishes. With accurate room information and confirmed products, the preview can achieve up to a 98% visual match to your proposed finished bathroom.

Can DLS visit and provide a quotation on the same day?

Subject to appointment availability, location and having enough project information, DLS can visit, assess the room and provide a quotation on the same day. Ask when you enquire and we will confirm what is possible for your postcode.

Is DLS bathroom work guaranteed?

DLS provides a 24-month workmanship guarantee from practical completion, alongside your statutory consumer rights.
