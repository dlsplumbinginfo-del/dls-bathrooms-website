A few useful details.

Can you recreate one of these bathrooms?

We can use a look as the starting point for your design and quotation. The final layout and products must suit your room, services, budget and available stock. AI images can vary from the exact shape, colour and scale of real products, so we confirm those details with you before ordering.

Where do the tiles come from?

Each look includes named Mandarin Stone tile suggestions under “Materials, tiles & lighting”. Those links let you see the actual range and request samples. Tile patterns and colours in the images are illustrative; physical samples and the supplier’s specifications take precedence.

Are the illuminated niches included as a product?

The niche and its lighting are planned as separate elements. We confirm a suitable niche, waterproofing and electrical lighting detail for the room. The glow shown in these images does not mean the niche is supplied with an integrated light.

Where can I see bathrooms DLS has actually completed?

Visit our completed-work gallery for photographs of real DLS installations. This inspiration collection is labelled separately because every image here is AI-generated.

How are my favourites saved?

Favourites are stored in this browser. Use “Copy shortlist link” to share them or return to the same selection on another device. Your shortlist is only sent to DLS when you choose to send your WhatsApp message.
