Privacy notice

How we use enquiry details.

This is a simple privacy notice for customers using the DLS Bathrooms enquiry form.

Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry we may receive your name, postcode, phone number, email address, project details and any photos or files you choose to upload.

Why we use it

We use this information to review your bathroom, tiling or plumbing enquiry, contact you about the project and provide or discuss a quotation.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the enquiry, quotation, project administration or related record-keeping.

Sharing

We do not sell your enquiry details. The website's enquiry form uses a form-delivery service to send submitted details to the DLS business email account.

Questions or removal requests

Contact DLS Bathrooms at dlstilingandplumbing30@gmail.com or call 07304 056595.
