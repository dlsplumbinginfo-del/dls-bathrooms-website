Remote bathroom estimate

Start with a clear video.

Send DLS a slow room walk-through, clear photographs and rough measurements through the main enquiry route.

What to send

Show every wall, the floor, ceiling, doors, windows, current fixtures and visible pipework. Include the room length, width and ceiling height where possible.

What happens next

DLS reviews the room, proposed layout, preferred finish and likely scope before confirming whether a provisional estimate or a home visit is the best next step.

Photos and video

Continue to the enquiry, complete the short project form, then attach the room photographs and video when WhatsApp opens. Large files can be sent in a follow-up message.

Important

A remote estimate is based on the information supplied. A final written quotation may change if measurements, product choices, access, hidden conditions or the agreed scope differ.
